Idle

Pinch (thumb + index) and drag on the grid to draw a base, release, then pinch with both hands and pull apart (or drag up with one pinching hand) to extrude. A closed fist grabs the scene and drags it with your hand (left / right / up / down) — roll your wrist while holding it to rotate the scene. With two fists, hold one still as the anchor: move the other away to zoom in, toward it to zoom out, or circle it to turn the scene; open palm stops. Committed meshes export as model.stl. Gesture events are logged to the console as JSON.
