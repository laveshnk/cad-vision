Idle

Switch modes with the buttons along the top of the camera overlay — click them, or point (index finger up, other fingers curled) and hold your fingertip on a button for half a second. VIEW: camera navigation only. SELECT: a quick pinch selects a mesh (pinch empty ground to deselect); point at the floating color wheel to repaint it — hold your fingertip on one hue for 1.2 seconds to lock it in; pinch and hold to move it, release to drop it; delete it with the floating Delete button (click it, pinch it, or point at it) or the Delete/Backspace key, then confirm. CREATE: pick a shape, pinch with both hands and pull apart to size the base, relax the upper pinch and drag the lower hand to set the height. A closed fist orbits the camera — roll your wrist to rotate. With two fists, move both: apart to zoom in, together to zoom out, or turn them like a steering wheel to turn the scene (moving only one fist just moves the camera). The camera thumbnail expands with its corner button and resizes by dragging the grip in its bottom-right corner. Committed meshes export as model.stl; gesture events are logged to the console as JSON.
