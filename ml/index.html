Idle

Switch modes with the buttons along the top of the camera overlay — click them, or hover with your index finger for half a second / pinch over a button. VIEW: camera navigation only. SELECT: pinch a mesh to pick it up and drag it along the ground (pinch empty ground to deselect). CREATE: pinch (thumb + index) and drag on the grid to draw a footprint, release, then pull apart with two pinching hands (or drag up with one) to extrude. A closed fist grabs the scene — move it to pan, roll your wrist to rotate. With two fists, hold one still as the anchor: move the other away to zoom in, toward it to zoom out, or circle it to turn the scene. Committed meshes export as model.stl; gesture events are logged to the console as JSON.
