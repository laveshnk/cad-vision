Idle

Switch modes with the buttons along the top of the camera overlay — click them, or point (index finger up, other fingers curled) and hold your fingertip on a button for half a second. VIEW: camera navigation only. SELECT: a quick pinch selects a mesh (pinch empty ground to deselect); point at the floating color wheel to repaint it; pinch and hold to move it, release to drop. CREATE: pick a shape, pinch with both hands and pull apart to size the base, relax the upper pinch and drag the lower hand to set the height. A closed fist orbits the camera — roll your wrist to rotate. With two fists, move both: apart to zoom in, together to zoom out, or turn them like a steering wheel to turn the scene (moving only one fist just moves the camera). Committed meshes export as model.stl; gesture events are logged to the console as JSON.
