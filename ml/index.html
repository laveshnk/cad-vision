Idle

Pinch (thumb + index) with both hands and pull apart to size the base at the origin (box: square, cuboid: rectangle, cylinder: circle). Relax the upper pinch and drag the lower hand up / down to set the height, then release; releasing the lower pinch first (or both at once) makes a flat plate. A closed fist orbits the camera around the origin — roll your wrist while holding it to rotate the scene. With two fists, hold one still as the anchor: move the other away to zoom in, toward it to zoom out, or circle it to turn the scene; open palm stops. Committed meshes export as model.stl. Gesture events are logged to the console as JSON.
